Expected impact

Scientific, social, clinical, and commercial outcomes

Scientific impact

New knowledge in biomaterial design, structure, and biological performance.

Social impact

RESBONREG is an interdisciplinary and innovative project aligned with national health programmes in Germany (Gesundheit für alle fördern) and European research strategies in health innovation. It addresses challenges in healthcare clusters by integrating regenerative medicine, biomaterials science, and biomedical engineering.

Clinical impact

Potential to support more predictable healing and regeneration outcomes.

Commercial impact

Translation potential for future medical and dental product development.
