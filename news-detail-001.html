Project kick-off meeting brings partners together in Graz

15 March 2026 ← Back to news
Project partners at the kick-off meeting in Graz working on the research plan.

Teams from Hochschule Rhein-Waal, TU Graz, and the Institute of Experimental Medicine CAS met to discuss project management, agree on fabrication protocols, and plan the first round of biological validation experiments.

Meeting Overview

The RESBONREG project consortium held its inaugural kick-off meeting on June 15, 2026, at the Institute of Biomechanics, Graz University of Technology (TU Graz), Austria. This brought together the consortium members to kick start the project, exchnages information and visit the infrastructure and laboratories at TU Graz. The meeting provided an opportunity to align on project objectives, formalise key expectations, meeting schedules and data management protocols, and discuss the initial research plans for the upcoming phases of the project.

The RESBONREG project is funded by the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG), Austrian Science Fund (FWF), and the Czech Science Foundation (GAČR), representing a strong commitment to advancing regenerative medicine research across Central Europe.
