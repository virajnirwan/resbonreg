RESBONREG

Electrically stimulated 3D Scaffold for Bone Regeneration

Developing advanced biomaterials to support improved tissue healing and regeneration.

About the Project

This project focuses on the development of innovative biomaterial-based solutions for tissue regeneration. The aim is to combine scientific research, material fabrication, and biological validation to create a platform with strong clinical and commercial potential.

The Problem

Many current treatment approaches still face limitations in predictable healing, material integration, and long-term performance. There is a need for advanced materials that can better support the natural healing process.

Our Solution

The project develops a new generation of structured biomaterials designed to guide tissue response, improve healing conditions, and support future translation into practical applications.

Technology and Innovation

The approach combines material design, advanced fabrication, and biological testing to create functional prototypes with controlled structure and performance.

Impact

The project has the potential to create scientific, clinical, and commercial value by supporting better healing outcomes and opening opportunities for future medical product development.
