Interdisciplinary research · Bone regeneration

Electrically stimulated 3D scaffolds for bone regeneration

RESBONREG aims to design and fabricate 3D hybrid nanostructured biomaterials that mimic the structural and functional characteristics of bone tissue. Controlled electrical stimulation (ES) is employed to promote cell proliferation, differentiation, migration, adhesion, and the secretion of growth factors.
