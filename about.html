About the project

Advancing biomaterial-based tissue regeneration

This project focuses on the development of innovative biomaterial-based solutions for guided tissue healing and regeneration. The work combines material design, fabrication, biological validation, and translation-oriented development across partner institutions in Germany, Austria, and the Czech Republic.

RESBONREG brings together expertise in biomaterials science, biomedical engineering, tissue engineering, and regenerative medicine to address challenges in bone healing and reconstruction. The consortium follows an interdisciplinary approach aligned with European health research priorities.
