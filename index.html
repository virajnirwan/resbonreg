About the Project

This project focuses on the development of innovative biomaterial-based solutions for guided tissue healing and regeneration. The work combines material design, fabrication, biological validation, and translation-oriented development.

Technology and Innovation

The project uses advanced fabrication strategies to create structured biomaterials with controlled properties. These materials are designed to support tissue response, improve healing conditions, and enable future product development.
