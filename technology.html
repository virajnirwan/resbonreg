Technology and innovation

Structured biomaterials with controlled biological response

The project uses advanced fabrication strategies to create structured biomaterials with controlled properties. These materials are designed to support tissue response, improve healing conditions, and enable future product development.

Hybrid nanostructured scaffolds are combined with controlled electrical stimulation to promote cell proliferation, differentiation, migration, adhesion, and growth factor secretion — key processes in bone regeneration.
