Electrically stimulated 3D Scaffold for Bone Regeneration

RESBONREG aims to design and fabricate 3D construct hybrid nanostructured biomaterials that mimic the structural and functional characteristics of bone tissues. Controlled electrical stimulation (ES) will be employed to promote cell proliferation, differentiation, migration, adhesion, and the secretion of growth factors.

About the Project

This project focuses on the development of innovative biomaterial-based solutions for guided tissue healing and regeneration. The work combines material design, fabrication, biological validation, and translation-oriented development.

Technology and Innovation

The project uses advanced fabrication strategies to create structured biomaterials with controlled properties. These materials are designed to support tissue response, improve healing conditions, and enable future product development.

Expected Impact

Scientific Impact

New knowledge in biomaterial design, structure, and biological performance.

Social Impact

RESBONREG is an interdisciplinary and innovative project aligned with national health programs in Germany (Public Health: Gesundheit für alle fördern) and European research strategies in health innovation to overcome challenges in healthcare clusters by integrating regenerative medicine, biomaterials science, and biomedical engineering.

Clinical Impact

Potential to support more predictable healing and regeneration outcomes.

Commercial Impact

Translation potential for future medical and dental product development.

Hochschuele Rhein Waal

Rhine-Waal University of Applied Sciences, with its two campus locations in Kleve and Kamp- Lintfort, is a university that is both deeply rooted in the Lower Rhine and connected to the world. Our 5,000 students, who represent altogether more than 120 different nationalities, are enrolled in 25 bachelor’s and eleven master’s degree programmes. The courses offered span the scientific spectrum, including engineering, technology, economics as well as social, natural and health sciences across four faculties and two campuses. As a university of applied sciences, the focus of degree programmes is on practical applications and real-world relevance. The students learn the important fundamentals of business economics and law, and are given numerous opportunities to develop interpersonal soft skills that are key for the business world, such as presentation and communication techniques, project management skills, social and intercultural skills and conflict management strategies. The university has close ties to business, research and academic institutions generate opportunities for researchers in joint R&D projects, and for students in the form of practical projects, internships and thesis topics.
